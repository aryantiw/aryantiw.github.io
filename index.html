IntentTabs

Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important. This extension is designed to work without collecting or sharing any personal data.

1. Data Collection

IntentTabs does not collect any personally identifiable information such as name, email address, or location.

2. Local Storage

All data, including tab intents and browsing context, is stored locally in your browser using the storage API. This data never leaves your device.

3. Permissions Usage

The extension uses browser permissions such as tabs and activeTab only to associate your intent with tabs and display helpful reminders.

4. No Tracking

IntentTabs does not track your browsing activity, does not use analytics, and does not send data to any servers.

5. Third-Party Sharing

No data is shared with third parties. Everything remains private and on your device.

6. Updates

This privacy policy may be updated if new features are added. Any changes will continue to respect user privacy.
